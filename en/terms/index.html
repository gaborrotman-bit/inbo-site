Who publishes inbo

Inbo is published by an individual, in a personal capacity: Gabor Rotman. Contact: contact@getinbo.ch.

What the app is for

Inbo helps you understand your letters and not forget their deadlines. The app reads the document and suggests a summary, an action, a date and how long to keep it.

Reading by an ai

To analyze your documents, Inbo has them read by an external AI (Claude, from Anthropic), only after your consent and according to the mode you choose. What is sent and where is described in the privacy policy.

General information, never advice

Inbo gives general information. It is never legal, tax, medical or financial advice, and the app does not replace a lawyer, accountant, doctor or adviser. For an important decision or a dispute, ask a professional, for example the tenants' association for housing.

The ai can make mistakes

The results come from artificial intelligence. They can be wrong or incomplete, including calculated deadlines. Always check dates, amounts and recipients on the original document. The document prevails.

Official sources prevail

The document lists for procedures and the links come from official sources, summarised. They can change or depend on your municipality. If they differ, the official body prevails: check the list with it.

No payments by the app

Inbo makes no payments and never has access to your accounts. The app only prepares the details. You pay yourself, in your banking app, after checking the amount and the recipient.

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for checking your deadlines, amounts and payments, and for doing your procedures on time. A reminder may not arrive, for example if notifications are off or the iPhone is switched off.

Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by Swiss law and applicable European Union law, the publisher is not liable for damage caused by an AI error, a wrong summary or deadline, a missed reminder or an interruption of the service, nor for indirect damage such as lost profit. This limitation does not apply to intentional misconduct or gross negligence, to harm to life or health, or where a mandatory law, such as consumer protection law in your country, provides for liability.

The subscription

Without a subscription, 25 documents are free, then 3 per month; every task stays visible and one complete task is free. Solo costs CHF 3.90 per month or CHF 34 per year, for one person. Household costs CHF 6.90 per month or CHF 59 per year, up to 6 people. Solo and Household include every task, tax return included: a subscriber never buys anything extra. The Tax Pack costs CHF 9.90, paid once for one tax year; it is only for accounts without a subscription and opens that year's tax return. The prices shown by Apple, in your account's currency, apply. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before, in your Apple account settings. Each plan's usage limits are shown in the app.

Normal use

The app is made for your own documents and those of your household. It is limited to 30 analyses per day. Only send documents you have the right to process.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Everything our server keeps about you is then erased, and your documents are erased from this iPhone.

Changes

If these terms change, the app asks you to accept them again. The date of your consent is recorded.

Governing law

These terms are governed by Swiss law. If you are a consumer living in the European Union, you keep the protection of the mandatory rules of your country and may go to the courts where you live. Contact: contact@getinbo.ch.

Inbo gives general information, never legal, tax or financial advice.
