Inbo : Privacy policy

12 November 2026

In short

Your documents stay on your iPhone. To understand them, Inbo has them read by an external AI, Claude, from Anthropic, after your consent. You choose what is sent with normal mode or confidential mode. Our server passes them on to the AI, then keeps neither your documents nor their text.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Gabor Rotman, a natural person in Switzerland. For any question about your data: contact@getinbo.ch.

What happens on your iPhone

Reading text, QR codes and identity documents, masking in confidential mode and reminders happen on your iPhone, without sending anything.

Normal mode (the most complete)

The AI reads the whole document, including handwriting. Sent: the full text read, with nothing masked, and reduced photos of the first four pages. Your names, your address, your AHV number or your IBAN may therefore be included.

Confidential mode (the most discreet)

Your name, your address and your numbers are masked on your iPhone before the AI reads the document. Handwriting is not taken into account, and some fine details (a ticked box, for example) may escape the analysis. Only two things are sent: the printed text, in which your name, your address, your numbers (AHV, IBAN, insured person, taxpayer), your email, your phone number, your date of birth and those of the people in your household are replaced by labels; and, if the iPhone judges it safe, the image of the sender's logo area. No page photo is sent. The table linking the labels to your real data stays on your iPhone.

The logo area

In confidential mode, the iPhone cuts out only the sender's logo, at the top of the first page, above the address window. It is never sent if it touches the address window, if the iPhone reads a name, an address, a number or an email in it, if it sees handwriting in it or small text right next to it, or for an identity document or a health document. When in doubt, it is not sent.

The limits of masking

Masking follows automatic rules. It can miss a piece of data, for example a name without a title in the middle of a sentence, or a misread word. The sender, the dates, the amounts and the references of the letter (invoice, customer or policy number) remain readable: they are needed to understand the letter.

What is also sent with an analysis

In both modes, technical data, separate from the content of your documents: the app language, the number of pages, a randomly generated identifier for the document, your country and your canton (where you live and where you work, if you have entered them), never your municipality or postcode, to calculate the correct deadlines, and an Apple DeviceCheck token, which says nothing about you and protects against abuse: it prevents the free documents from being claimed again by reinstalling the app. If you created your own binders or tasks: their name and what you want to put in them, so the AI can file the document there; masked in confidential mode.

Other features

Sorting a multi-page scan: the text of each page and clues (page number, references, date, subject), masked in confidential mode; in normal mode, also a small photo of each page. Searching for a few words happens on your iPhone, without sending anything. Ask your documents: your question and, for the chosen documents, the sender, the type, the dates, the amount, the status, the folder, the summary and a few lines of those that contain the words of your question, never the whole text; in confidential mode, your question and these lines are masked and a document analyzed in that mode is sent with its masked texts; codes, IBANs and AHV numbers are always removed. Creating a binder or a task: its name and what you want to put in it are sent the same way, to find the documents that could go there. Writing a reply: the text of the letter, always masked, and what you want to reply, in a few words, also masked; the letter is completed on your iPhone. Linking two documents, when a doubt remains: the type, the sender, the date, the amount and the summary of both, masked for a document analyzed in confidential mode.

Your ID documents

Your ID card, your passport and your permits are recognized and read only on your iPhone. Neither their photo nor their text is sent, in any mode.

Your QR-bills

The QR code is read on your iPhone and is never sent as is. In normal mode, the photo of the page it is printed on is sent with the document, like the rest of the page.

Your consent for the AI

Before the first analysis, the app asks for your consent to reading by an external AI. Without it, nothing is sent to the AI. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings. Inbo will then no longer be able to analyse new documents.

Who it goes to, and where

Our server: Supabase, whose database is in Zurich, Switzerland. It receives what the app sends, passes it on to the AI and returns the answer. The AI: Anthropic (Claude). The contract is concluded with Anthropic Ireland Limited, and processing takes place in the United States. This transfer is based on the standard contractual clauses of Anthropic's data processing agreement, adapted for Switzerland. Anthropic does not train its models on this data. Apple: Sign in with Apple, the DeviceCheck token and, if you keep it turned on, the iCloud backup. Expo, in the United States (certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework): when the app checks for an update, it sends your IP address, a randomly generated installation identifier and the app version, never anything from your documents. RevenueCat, in the United States: only if you subscribe, the status of your subscription with your Inbo account identifier; payment goes through Apple.

How long

At Anthropic: deleted after 30 days at most, unless content is flagged for a breach of its rules (up to 2 years) or the law requires it to be kept. On our server, never any document content. The technical log of each request (the kind of request, the step, the result, an error code, the mode, the length of the text, the number of photos and the duration): 30 days. Tracking of blockages and the beta diagnostics: 30 days. Your account (an identifier, the email address provided by Apple, which you can hide, and a token used to revoke the sign-in with Apple), your usage counters (a randomly generated identifier per counted document, never its content; the questions and letters of the day or month), your plan, your referral, the free task you chose (its name only)s and your acceptance of the terms: until your account is deleted. The cost of each AI call (model, number of tokens, price): when your account is deleted, the link to you is erased, and the totals remain for accounting.

What stays on your iPhone

The photos and PDFs, the text read, the table linking the labels to your real data, the results, the content of QR bills and your name if you entered it. They are protected by the iPhone's encryption: unreadable when it is locked. By default, they are part of your iPhone's iCloud backup and of Apple's transfer to a new iPhone; with Apple's Advanced Data Protection, this copy is end-to-end encrypted. You can exclude them from the backup in Inbo's settings.

Changing iPhone

"I'm changing iPhones" sends your documents, their pages, your household and your settings directly from your old iPhone to the new one, over the local network or Bluetooth. Everything is encrypted with a key contained in the QR code shown on the old iPhone: only the iPhone that scans it can read it. Nothing goes through our server, and nothing is kept anywhere else.

Share to Inbo

From Mail, WhatsApp, Safari, Files or Photos, "Share" then "Inbo" copies the PDF or image into the app, on your iPhone. Nothing goes through our server before the analysis, which then follows your mode, as for a scan.

Exporting your documents

Exporting a task, a binder, a section, one person's documents or all of Inbo creates a ZIP file (or a PDF) on your iPhone, then opens the Share menu: you choose where it goes. Nothing goes through our server.

Your calendar and reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, without a server. By default, your due dates stay in the app. If you choose a calendar on your iPhone, the event contains only the document type and the sender. This calendar may be synced by its provider, such as iCloud, Outlook or Gmail.

Detecting freezes

For all accounts, the app notes when a wait lasts more than a minute, when it is closed during an analysis, or when a card does not open after the analysis. Only the kind of blockage, the step, the duration, the time, the analysis mode and the app version are sent, never a text, a name, an amount or a number. Deleted after 30 days.

The optional diagnostic (beta)

During the beta, the app asks you only once whether you agree to send technical diagnostics about your documents: their status, the filled or empty fields, the steps, the errors and the deadlines in days, never their content, a date, a name, an amount or a number. You can turn it off at any time in Settings: what has been sent is then deleted. Otherwise, everything is deleted after 30 days.

The beta test

If you test the app before its public release, our server keeps your confidentiality agreement: the date and the version of the text. If you fill in the end-of-test questionnaire, it also keeps your answers: your ratings, your choices and what you wrote, never a document. They are deleted with your account.

No ads, no tracking

Inbo shows no ads, sells no data and does not track you from one app to another.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and receive it. Deleting your account, in Settings, erases everything our server keeps about you, except the costs, which are no longer linked to you. Your documents are on your iPhone: deleting them, or deleting the app, erases them. Write to contact@getinbo.ch, reply within 30 days. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Changes

If this policy changes, its date changes, and the app asks you to accept the terms and the policy again.

Inbo gives general information, never legal, tax or financial advice.
