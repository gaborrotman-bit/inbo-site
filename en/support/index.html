Frequently asked questions

Where are my documents kept?

On your iPhone. Your documents, their photos and their results stay there; our server keeps neither your documents nor their text. To understand them, Inbo has them read by an external AI (Claude, by Anthropic), only after you agree. Anthropic deletes them after 30 days at most. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

What is the difference between normal and confidential mode?

In normal mode, the photo of the pages is sent, which gives the best results (handwriting included). In confidential mode, your name, your address and your numbers are masked on your iPhone before sending, and no page photo is sent: only the masked printed text, and sometimes the image of the sender’s logo. You change mode in Settings, Analysis.

How do I pay a QR bill?

Open the bill in Inbo and, under “Pay this bill”, tap your app’s button. Inbo prepares the bill for your banking app (sharing the PDF, or saving the QR code to Photos, depending on your bank). You check and confirm in your banking app. Inbo never makes a payment and never has access to your accounts.

I don’t get a reminder before a deadline.

Check that notifications are allowed for Inbo (iPhone Settings, Notifications, Inbo) and that “Get reminded before each deadline” is on in Inbo’s Settings. A reminder may not arrive if the iPhone is off: keep checking important deadlines yourself.

The AI got a date or an amount wrong.

On the document’s page, tap the date, the sender, the person or the binder to correct them. An amount is corrected in your banking app, before confirming. The AI can make mistakes; the original document always prevails.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Your documents are not in the iCloud backup unless you turned it on in Settings. Otherwise: on the old iPhone, Settings, “I’m changing iPhones” shows a code; on the new one, “Recover from my old iPhone” scans it. Everything goes directly from one iPhone to the other, without our server.

How much does Inbo cost?

Without a subscription: 25 documents free, then 3 a month, and one complete paperwork guide free. Solo, for one person: CHF 3.90 a month or CHF 34 a year. Household, up to 6 people: CHF 6.90 a month or CHF 59 a year. The Tax Pack (CHF 9.90, once per tax year) unlocks that year’s tax return without a subscription. Prices in the app come from Apple, in your currency.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Apple manages the subscription: iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Inbo. Or in Inbo: Settings, Subscription, “Manage my subscription”. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. For a refund, contact Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

I changed iPhone and my subscription is missing.

Open the subscription screen and tap Restore, with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I delete my account?

In Inbo: Settings, “Delete my account and my data”. Everything our server keeps about you is erased, and your documents are erased from this iPhone. If you have a subscription, cancel it with Apple too. You can also ask for deletion by e-mail.

Can two people use Inbo on two iPhones?

Household gives one profile per person, up to six, on the same iPhone. Sharing documents between two iPhones does not exist yet.

Which countries and languages?
